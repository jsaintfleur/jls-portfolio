Data & ethics

Credibility is the product. Every number traces to a public source, and every limit is stated.

  • Only credible public data — Census, HUD, CDC, FRED, CFPB, MTA, FEMA, NOAA, NYC Open Data, and documented research datasets, each verified for source, license, and coverage.
  • Licenses honored — non-commercial and attribution terms (County Health Rankings, Eviction Lab, Olist) are flagged in each repo's DATA_SOURCES.md and respected.
  • Computed vs. proposed — every app clearly separates what is calculated from what is a planned enhancement. No fabricated data, users, metrics, or impact.
  • Honest about limits — models report where they lose to a baseline; data-sparse geographies are excluded and counted; margins of error and modeled estimates are surfaced, not hidden.
  • Reproducible — each pipeline rebuilds its data with one command and validates the output against a schema gate in CI.
