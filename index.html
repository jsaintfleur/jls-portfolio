Integrity guardrails

Credibility is the product.

Every number traces to a public source, every license flag is honored, and every limit stays visible.

Data & ethics

  • Only credible public data: Census, HUD, CDC, FRED, CFPB, MTA, FEMA, NOAA, NYC Open Data, and documented research datasets.
  • Licenses honored: non-commercial and attribution terms are flagged in each repo and respected.
  • Computed vs. proposed: calculated outputs stay separate from planned enhancements. No fabricated metrics or impact.
  • Honest limits: data-sparse geographies, modeled estimates, and baseline comparisons are surfaced.
  • Reproducible where repo-backed: portfolio pipelines rebuild with one command and validate output against schema gates.
